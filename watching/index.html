Connect NovaShow

  1. Open NovaShow or another streaming site in a new tab or window.
  2. Drag the Share with opieferd link to your bookmarks bar.
  3. Open the show/episode you’re watching and click that bookmark.

Works with NovaShow, Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Max, Prime Video, Paramount+, Peacock, Crunchyroll, and most sites that show the title and episode in the page. Nothing is sent to a server: the bookmarklet reads the current page and its browser storage, then sends the result directly back here.
