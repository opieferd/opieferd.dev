GOOD TO KNOW

The little details.

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, and Deezer open in their official embedded players. Albums and playlists stay inside those players; queue skip moves between links, not individual songs inside an album. The transport controls can directly operate browser audio and supported YouTube/SoundCloud widgets; other services use their own controls. Direct audio URLs and locally selected files play using your browser's audio controls.

Streaming services decide whether full songs, embeds, and offline playback are available. The external downloader option is a convenience handoff, not a built-in MP3 extractor or a guarantee of downloads.
